# User Ideas

Your feature ideas, written by hand. Write them as short or vague as you like: a title is
enough. `/plan-features` reads them, asks you what it needs to know, and turns them into
concrete features in `CLAUDE_PLAN.md` (deleting the idea here once it is planned, or
narrowing it to what is left). Nothing here is built directly. Rules (ids, stamps) are in
`list-format.md` of the `dev-workflow` plugin's `setup-workflow` skill.

Claude adds ideas here only when you approve one of its own suggestions from
`CLAUDE_IDEAS.md` (`/pitch-feature`). A feature's **Keep open for:** line in `CLAUDE_PLAN.md`
may name ideas here that its implementation must not block.

**[HOLD]** after the severity means: don't plan this idea until the tag is removed.

Legend (optional): **[H]** most wanted, **[M]** wanted, **[L]** nice to have.
