# Claude Ideas

Claude's own feature suggestions, written by `/suggest-features`. Nothing here is planned or
built without your approval: `/pitch-feature` pitches one and, if you approve it, moves it to
`USER_IDEAS.md`; `/plan-features claude:<id>` plans one directly. Rules (ids, stamps, deleting
done items) are in `list-format.md` of the `dev-workflow` plugin's `setup-workflow` skill. All
paths are relative to `<code root>` unless noted. Items are roughly ordered by priority.

Every idea has to fit the design docs named in `USER_WORKFLOW.md`. **[CONFLICT]** marks the
rare idea that contradicts them; it says what it conflicts with and why it is worth adding
anyway. **[HOLD]** after the severity means: don't pitch this idea until the tag is removed.

The one exception to "only open items" is the **Dropped** section at the end: ideas you
turned down, one line each with the reason, so later passes don't suggest them again.

Passes:

Legend: **[H]** big improvement of the core loop, **[M]** clear improvement, **[L]** nice to
have.
